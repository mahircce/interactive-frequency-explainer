What is Frequency?

Simply put, frequency is how often something happens over a specific amount of time. Usually, we measure this in "events per second", known as Hertz (Hz).

Blinking Light

The simplest example. A frequency of 1 Hz means the light flashes exactly once every second. Currently flashing 1 time per second.

Swinging Pendulum

Physical movement. One full cycle (back and forth) is one event. Completing 1 cycle per second.

Data / Sound Wave

How signals travel. Higher frequency means more waves fit into the same time space (higher pitch in sound). 1 peak passing per second.

Hear Frequency (Pitch)

In audio, frequency determines pitch. Human hearing ranges from roughly 20 Hz (very low rumble) to 20,000 Hz (piercing high pitch).
